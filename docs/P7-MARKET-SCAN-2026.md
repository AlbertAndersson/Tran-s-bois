# P7 – Market scan supporter assortment 2026-10-04

## Purpose

This note documents the external market inspiration behind site/commerce/supporter-preview.html.
It is an idea bank for Tranås BoIS, not a supplier decision, product approval, price list or launch authorization.

The existing commercial gate remains **2027-01-01**. No product on the preview page is orderable merely because it is shown there.

## Official club-shop observations

### Villa Lidköping BK
Source:
- https://vlbk.se/2024/03/10/kla-dig-infor-sm-finalerna-klubblokalen-forvandlas-till-supportershop/
- https://vlbk.se/nyhetsarkiv/page/5/

Observed assortment themes:
- clothes
- match jerseys
- beanies
- scarves
- wigs / match-day visual items
- final/event-specific shop activation

Relevance for BoIS:
Bandy-specific match-day merchandise is credible and can be activated around finals, derbies and other events.

### Leksands IF
Source:
- https://shop.leksandsif.se/
- https://shop.leksandsif.se/allproducts
- https://shop.leksandsif.se/startsida/start-matchdag

Observed assortment themes include:
- match jerseys with and without custom print
- hoodies, sweatshirts, fleece, T-shirts
- scarves, beanies and caps
- mugs, thermos mugs, bottles and glassware
- flags
- keyrings, lanyards, stickers and magnets
- bags and cooler bags
- baby/kids apparel
- puzzles, card decks and other gift products
- limited and retro concepts

Relevance for BoIS:
A club shop can support both a focused core assortment and a long-tail gift assortment.

### AIK
Source:
- https://www.aikshop.se/sv/articles/413/halsdukar
- https://www.aikshop.se/sv/articles/783/mossor

Observed assortment themes:
- many scarf designs
- many beanie designs
- combined kids sets
- anniversary / section / identity-specific variants

Relevance for BoIS:
The same basic product type can support multiple limited designs without changing the commerce model.

### Malmö FF
Source:
- https://www.mffshopen.se/accessoarer
- https://www.mffshopen.se/accessoarer/halsdukar/halsdukar
- https://www.mffshopen.se/barn/baby/mossor

Observed assortment themes:
- scarves, beanies, caps and gloves
- bracelets/necklaces
- watches
- keyrings/lanyards
- pins, patches and stickers
- bags
- stationery
- magnets and sunglasses
- baby and junior products

Relevance for BoIS:
Accessories and family products broaden the shop beyond apparel while keeping many products one-size.

### HV71 / Intersport
Source:
- https://www.intersport.se/supporterklubbar/hv71
- https://www.intersport.se/hv71

Observed assortment themes:
- match jerseys
- T-shirts
- mugs
- scarves
- beanies
- unusual club-branded niche products, including a fishing lure

Relevance for BoIS:
A club can credibly sell locally relevant niche products in addition to standard merchandise. This supports exploring bandy-specific BoIS products such as a branded bandy ball or skate guards.

## Recommended assortment strategy for Erik discussion

### Start
Prefer products with clear supporter demand and limited operational complexity:
- scarf
- beanie
- supporter T-shirt
- hoodie
- cap
- mug
- keyring/lanyard
- hand flag

### Expand after demand is proven
- sweatshirt
- zip hoodie
- fleece
- neck warmer
- gloves
- socks
- bucket hat
- supporter replica jersey
- thermos mug
- bottle
- sport bag
- cooler bag
- sticker/patch
- magnet
- glassware
- puzzle/card deck

### Family
- kids T-shirt
- kids beanie/scarf set
- baby body
- plush toy
- junior hoodie
- knatte pack

### Limited / campaign
- retro jersey
- member-exclusive personalized scarf
- signed jersey
- match-worn item
- final/derby/jubilee product
- BoIS branded bandy ball
- skate guards
- gift card only after accounting/redemption flow is designed

## Commercial safety

Every future orderable P7 product must still have:
- BoIS approval
- selected supplier
- verified supplier SKU/variant mapping
- purchase price and decoration cost
- Swedish delivery/freight handling
- sale price and VAT handling
- margin decision
- stock or preorder/on-demand strategy
- product image/design approval
- return policy
- confirmed launch date

Market examples are inspiration only. Prices and supplier claims from other clubs must not be copied as BoIS commercial facts.
